Our Story

Three Sisters, One Dream

Since we were young girls, we always dreamed of opening a cafe together. "How cool would it be to have a cafe that sells coffee, flowers, and books all at the same place?" we used to wonder.

Years passed, but our dream never faded. We imagined a cozy place where the rich aroma of freshly brewed coffee would mingle with the sweet scent of flowers, while shelves filled with carefully curated books invite you to escape into different worlds.

Trio Cafe is that dream realized - a space where you can enjoy premium coffee, discover your next favorite book, and take home beautiful floral arrangements, all under one roof. Each of us brings our passion: one for the perfect coffee blend, one for literary treasures, and one for floral artistry.

Our cafe is more than just a business; it's a labor of love, a testament to sisterhood, and a sanctuary for those seeking comfort, inspiration, and beauty in everyday moments. We believe in creating experiences that nourish both body and soul.

Welcome to Trio Cafe - where our childhood dream becomes your favorite reality.
